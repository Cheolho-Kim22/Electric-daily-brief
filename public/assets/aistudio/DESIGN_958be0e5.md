## Brand & Style

The design system establishes an authoritative, high-clarity editorial environment engineered for professionals and job candidates across power generation, energy utilities, and grid infrastructure. The visual posture merges the rigor of institutional energy intelligence with the immediacy of a modern financial terminal.

### Aesthetic Foundation
- **Modern Institutional Editorial**: Blends structural editorial weight with clean, data-dense web layouts. High visual discipline replaces decorative noise.
- **Electric Precision**: Deep industrial blues provide structural gravitas, accented by calibrated amber and high-frequency electric blue tokens to signify status, live signals, and essential takeaways.
- **High-Contrast Legibility**: Clear typographical contrast ensures that complex industry briefs can be digested in under five minutes.

### Audience & Mood
- **Target Audience**: Energy market analysts, Korea Power Exchange (KPX) and KEPCO applicants, power systems engineers, and industry professionals.
- **Emotional Response**: Confident, technically grounded, timely, and free of friction. Information hierarchy prioritizes signal-to-noise ratio above all else.

## Colors

The color palette anchors information hierarchy using high-density chromatic roles:

- **Primary (`#0A2540`)**: Institutional Midnight Navy. Governs root headers, primary typographic weights, core navigational framing, and high-impact structural panels.
- **Secondary (`#2563EB`)**: Signal Electric Blue. Assigned to interactive targets, active tabs, text links, selected states, and progressive disclosure toggles.
- **Accent Tokens**:
  - `volt-amber` (`#F59E0B`) and `amber-vivid` (`#EAB308`): Dedicated to AI synthesis indicators, live broadcast badges, attention-grabbing bookmarks, and alert banners.
  - `electric-blue-dark` (`#1E40AF`): Applied to button hover states, category pill hover strokes, and deep contrast links.
  - `energy-cyan` (`#0EA5E9`): Technical metric callouts, timeline pips, and sub-category cues.
- **Neutrals**:
  - Ground (`neutral-slate-50`, `#F8FAFC`): Provides a cool, anti-fatigue canvas.
  - Surface (`surface-card`, `#FFFFFF`): Crisp elevated paper tier for cards, side sheets, and drawers.
  - Borders (`neutral-slate-200`, `#E2E8F0`): Delivers razor-sharp edge definition without visual clutter.
  - Body Typography (`neutral-slate-900`, `#0F172A`): Ensures a minimum 9:1 contrast ratio against card backgrounds.
  - Meta/Subdued Text (`neutral-slate-600`, `#475569`): Retains readability for timestamps, reporter bylines, and auxiliary labels.

## Typography

The type scale is optimized for high-volume Korean text reading mixed with technical Latin acronyms (e.g., SMP, REC, ESS, K-ETS, LNG).

### Font Stack & Rules
- **Stack Hierarchy**: `Inter, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Pretendard", "Noto Sans KR", sans-serif`. `Inter` handles alphanumeric data and metrics, while `Pretendard` acts as the native drop-in Korean humanist sans-serif.
- **Word Break & Line Height**:
  - Apply `word-break: keep-all;` globally across all Korean body and header tags to avoid awkward breaks in multi-syllable terms.
  - AI 3-line summaries enforce a 1.625 line height multiplier (`body-lg` / `body-md`) to ensure comfortable vertical scanning.
- **Numerical Treatment**: Data callouts, timestamps, and ranking indices utilize `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1` for tabular alignment across compact cards.

## Layout & Spacing

The system uses an asymmetric, desktop-first workspace layout suited for analytical digestion within a five-minute daily window.

### Layout Mechanics
- **Max Width**: 1440px desktop shell pinned to center, standard 1280px minimum baseline for zero-reflow multi-pane viewing.
- **Split Workspace (Desktop > 1024px)**:
  - **Main Stream Area (Left / Center)**: 12-column responsive grid or dynamic flex container with `1.5rem` (`gutter`) spacing.
  - **Slide-over / Inspector Sheet (Right)**: 440px to 480px persistent or overlay side pane dedicated to deep article synthesis, full 3-sentence extraction, and outbound source jump.
- **Article Grid Configuration**:
  - `Desktop (>= 1280px)`: 2-column or 3-column masonry/structured card matrix.
  - `Tablet (768px - 1023px)`: 2-column grid; slide-over converts into a 50% width docking sheet.
  - `Mobile (< 768px)`: Single-column linear layout. The slide-over becomes a full-viewport bottom sheet or sliding screen with full touch gestures.
- **Spacing Cadence**: All component interior padding relies on multiples of `0.25rem` (`4px`), centering on `space-md` (`16px`) for cards and `space-lg` (`24px`) for section boundary blocks.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is built through structured surface tinting combined with subtle, high-clarity edge borders rather than heavy atmospheric drops.

### Elevation Hierarchy
1. **Canvas Level (Elevation 0)**: `#F8FAFC`. Base viewport background providing visual comfort and high contrast against white modules.
2. **Card Base Tier (Elevation 1)**: `#FFFFFF` paired with an explicit `1px solid #E2E8F0` border. No drop shadow in resting state.
3. **Hover & Interactive Float (Elevation 2)**: Maintained for interactive article cards. Elevates with `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.08)` and border shifts to `#2563EB` (or `#CBD5E1`).
4. **Slide-Over Inspection Panel (Elevation 3)**: Fixed side-sheet with `box-shadow: -8px 0 24px -4px rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.12)`. When treated as an overlay modal on smaller screens, it pairs with a `rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.4)` backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`).
5. **Hero Daily Digest Card (Featured Tier)**: Linear boundary accent: a subtle top gradient stroke `linear-gradient(90deg, #F59E0B 0%, #2563EB 100%)` with a `2px` top border to immediately identify the AI daily wrap-up.

## Shapes

The design system enforces a sharp, architectural radius profile (`roundedness: 1`). Soft 4px (`rounded-sm`) to 8px (`rounded-lg`) corner geometry establishes an analytical, paper-derived character suited for daily briefing cards.

### Radius Assignments
- **Chips, Category Pills & Meta Badges**: 4px radius (`0.25rem`). Avoid full pill shapes (50%) to retain an authoritative newsroom aesthetic.
- **Article & Summary Cards**: 8px radius (`0.5rem`). Clean, balanced rectangular framing.
- **Input Fields & Search Bars**: 6px to 8px radius (`0.375rem` - `0.5rem`).
- **Interactive Action Buttons**: 6px radius (`0.375rem`).
- **Slide-over Panel**: Flush right-hand border (`0px`) with 0px corner on edge docking, or `12px` top corners when docked as a bottom sheet on mobile screens.

## Components

### 1. Daily Core Summary Box (Hero 3-Line Module)
- **Container**: White background with a `2px` top accent rule (`#F59E0B`). Internal padding: `space-lg` (`24px`).
- **Header**: Contains the "오늘의 핵심 브리핑" label with an amber lightning badge (`label-sm`, uppercase), timestamp (`label-md` in `neutral-slate-600`), and an "AI 종합 분석" indicator.
- **Summary Rows**: 3 ordered bullet items styled with `body-lg`. Each bullet features a bold numerical glyph (`01`, `02`, `03`) tinted in `secondary_color_hex` (`#2563EB`).

### 2. Article Briefing Cards
- **Structure**: Surface white, `1px solid #E2E8F0` border, `16px` or `20px` internal padding.
- **Top Row**: Category chip (e.g., "에너지", "탄소중립") aligned left; Bookmark toggle button aligned right.
- **Title**: `headline-md`, clamped to 2 lines max. Hover changes color to `secondary_color_hex` (`#2563EB`).
- **3-Sentence Summary Preview**: Displayed in `body-md` (`neutral-slate-600`), clamped to 3 lines with subtle bullet dividers or a numbered vertical stack.
- **Tag Cloud**: Compact row of keyword tags (e.g., `#SMP`, `#전력망확충`).
- **Card Action**: Clicking the card triggers the Slide-Over Article Inspector Panel.

### 3. Badges, Category Chips & Keyword Tags
- **Category Chips**:
  - Inactive: Background `#F1F5F9`, text `#475569`, `roundedness: 1`, border `1px solid transparent`.
  - Active: Background `#0A2540`, text `#FFFFFF`, font weight `600`.
- **Keyword Tags**: Distinct technical styling with subtle energy accent. `#EFF6FF` (light blue) background, `#1E40AF` text, with a `#DBEAFE` border. Hover turns background to `#DBEAFE`.
- **Rank Indicator**: Bold, compact number indicator (e.g., `01`, `02`) placed on the top-left of top-tier stories.

### 4. Slide-Over Detail Inspector
- **Dimensions**: `480px` fixed width on desktop, docking to right screen edge. Full height `100vh`.
- **Content Stack**:
  - Close button and external source shortcut button in the sticky top header.
  - Section title, publishing timestamp, and original article rank badge.
  - Full article title (`headline-lg`).
  - Dedicated **3문장 요약** container wrapped in a `#F8FAFC` tonal frame with an amber spark icon.
  - **핵심 키워드 모음**: Large clickable keyword pills.
  - **원문 보기 Button**: Full-width primary CTA button with external link icon.

### 5. Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary CTA**: `#0A2540` background, `#FFFFFF` text. Hover shifts to `#1E40AF`.
- **Secondary / Ghost Button**: Transparent background, `#0A2540` border and text. Hover introduces `#F1F5F9` background.
- **Bookmark Icon Button**:
  - Unsaved: `#94A3B8` icon stroke. Hover turns to `#0A2540`.
  - Saved: `#F59E0B` fill with `#F59E0B` stroke.

### 6. Search & Filter Bar
- **Input Field**: Height `44px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`. Placeholder color `#94A3B8`. Focus rings shift to `2px solid #2563EB` with zero offset.
- **Integrated Icon**: Search magnifier glyph nested at left padding `12px`. Clear (`x`) button visible when string is active.

### 7. Notice & Fallback Banner
- **Warning / Fallback Banner (System Update Delay)**:
  - Displayed when today's crawl is pending or processing.
  - Background: `#FEF3C7` (amber tint).
  - Border: `1px solid #FCD34D`.
  - Content: Exclamation/clock icon in `#D97706` alongside text: *"오늘의 전기 뉴스 요약이 아직 준비되지 않았어요. 어제(YYYY.MM.DD) 요약을 보여드릴게요."*
  - Action: Optional refresh button on the trailing side.
